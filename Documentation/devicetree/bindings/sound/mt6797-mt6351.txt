MT6797 with MT6351 CODEC

Required properties:
- compatible: "mediatek,mt6797-mt6351-sound", or
  "planet,gemini-mt6797-mt6351" for the Gemini PDA capture gate
- mediatek,platform: the phandle of MT6797 ASoC platform
- mediatek,audio-codec: the phandles of MT6351 codec

Required properties for "planet,gemini-mt6797-mt6351":
- microphone-enable-gpios: active-high GPIO enabling the board's microphone
  capture path. This is the legacy headphone_en signal (GPIO234), not the
  headphone contact-selection signal or either speaker amplifier enable.
  It is held low while the card is idle and managed with the capture PGAs.

Example:

	sound {
		compatible = "mediatek,mt6797-mt6351-sound";
		mediatek,audio-codec = <&mt6351_snd>;
		mediatek,platform = <&afe>;
	};
